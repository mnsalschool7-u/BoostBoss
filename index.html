WHY PEQUOD EXISTS

Trade is complicated.
The next step shouldn’t be.

Pequod began with TerraLuxCraftworks and a shipment held at the Port of Colombo.

Importing Himalayan salt products from Pakistan exposed a difficult reality: when cargo stops moving, the information needed to resolve it is scattered across documents, broker emails, tariff schedules, and freight systems.

Pequod brings that context together, so importers and customs professionals can review what matters and decide what comes next.

Built around the questions an importer actually has to answer.
