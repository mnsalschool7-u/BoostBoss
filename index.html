THE BORDER PROBLEM

Know why your shipment is stuck. Know what to do next.

Pequod AI helps importers and exporters understand customs, classification, tariff, and documentation issues before they compound into delays, fees, and trapped inventory.

Every imported product needs a classification. That decision helps determine the applicable duties, tariffs, and regulatory requirements. But products do not always fit neatly into a single category.

When a shipment gets held, teams move between customs notices, tariff schedules, classification databases, broker emails, invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, and government guidance just to understand what went wrong.

Why is my shipment not moving?

Every additional day can mean demurrage, detention, storage charges, delayed inventory, and capital tied up in goods a business cannot access.

THE PEQUOD ANSWER

From shipment uncertainty to an actionable next step.

Pequod's broader mission is an intelligent coordination layer for trade operations.

Customs and broker notices only tell part of the story. Product facts, origin evidence, shipment documents, tariff context, and logistics constraints need to be considered together.

The classification workspace and ShipSmart are the current entry points into that workflow: inspect the evidence behind a customs decision, then compare the cost and timing of delivery.

  1. 01 / GATHER

    Upload the context

    Start with product documentation. The wider shipment context includes invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, certificates of origin, and customs or broker notices.

  2. 02 / STRUCTURE

    Bring the facts together

    Document intelligence organizes classification relevant product facts and preserves source evidence so the original information remains inspectable.

  3. 03 / REVIEW

    Understand the issue

    Review missing inputs, relevant CBP precedent, and candidate HTSUS analysis. Identify where a conclusion needs additional evidence or professional review.

  4. 04 / ACT

    Prepare the next decision

    Use the evidence in a broker discussion. Compare shipping quotes against the delivery deadline and account for packaging and selling fees with ShipSmart.

WHY PEQUOD EXISTS

Trade is complicated.
The next step shouldn’t be.

I got involved in TradeTech through TerraLuxCraftworks, my Himalayan pink salt company.

I import products including Himalayan pink salt lamps, candles, foot detoxers, and urns directly from suppliers in Pakistan.

When I started TerraLux, I assumed the hardest part would be finding customers or navigating Amazon. It was not. One of the hardest moments came when a six figure cargo shipment became unexpectedly delayed at the Port of Colombo in Sri Lanka.

Every additional day meant more demurrage and detention exposure, delayed inventory, and capital trapped inside goods I could not access. Working backward through the shipment forced me to understand how customs duties, classification, and tariff treatment could materially change the economics of a transaction.

Why is it still so difficult for businesses to understand what a shipment will cost, what customs expects, and what to do when something goes wrong?

That question became Pequod.

ShipSmart extends that same practical focus to a seller's next decision: how to get a heavy, fragile order to its buyer within a narrow delivery window, without overpaying for the label.
